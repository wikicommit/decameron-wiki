*Boccaccian experimentalism* denotes the versatility with which [[Person/giovanni-boccaccio]] handles the literary material he comes into contact with, reworking it and producing new original works. Some scholars, among them [[Person/vittore-branca]], define him for this reason as the greatest European prose writer of his time, a versatile writer who amalgamated different literary tendencies and genres, bringing them together in original works through a creative activity carried out under the sign of experimentalism.

## Use

The phenomenon manifests itself as early as the Neapolitan period. In the cosmopolitan climate of [[Place/naples]], where French chivalric ethics imported by the Angevins, Arab-Byzantine influences, courtly erudition and the presence of devotees of Dante's memory all meet, Boccaccio gives rise to an experimentalism in which all these elements intersect. Taking for example the [[Book/filocolo]], the first novel in the Italian vernacular, one may note that the title is a Grecism, that the narrative takes up the love story of Florio and Blancheflour bound to the Occitan tradition, and that classicising influences after the model of the *Historia distructionis Troiae* by Guido delle Colonne are at work in it.

The same attitude is reflected in the language. With the narrative fostered by Boccaccio, Italian literary prose reaches an extremely high level: thanks to his vernacular translation of Livy he adopts a more fluent period, less paratactic and centred on the hierarchical concatenation of clauses, a fluid and flowing style steeped in a language proper to the everyday dimension, made still more marked by the presence of dialectal forms and by contexts dominated by double meanings. Boccaccio's Latin, too, takes on a "hybrid" character with respect to the purity sought by [[Person/petrarch]]: although it becomes more expository and clear after his engagement with Livy's work and with Petrarch's prose, Boccaccio retained medievalising forms when he judged them useful for expressing certain concepts with precision.
