The *Introduction* to the Tenth Day is the section that opens the last of the ten days of the [[Book/decameron]] and precedes the First Tale. It is among the briefest in the work and has no narrative matter of its own; it contains, however, an allusion that distinguishes it from the other introductions, because for the first time the [[DefinedTerm/brigata]] discourses of what awaits it after the retreat.

## Contents

Certain little clouds are still red in the west, while those of the east have become most lucent and "at their edges like gold" from the sun's rays drawing near them, when [[Person/panfilo]] rises and has the women and his companions called. All being come, and it having been resolved together where they might go for their delight, the king sets out ahead at a slow pace, accompanied by [[Person/filomena]] and by [[Person/fiammetta]], followed by all the rest.

Speaking together "of many things concerning their future life", saying and answering, they go about diverting themselves for a long space; then, a very long circuit having been made and the sun already beginning to grow too warm, they return to the palace. Here, about the clear fountain, they have the glasses rinsed and whoever wishes drinks a little; then they take their solace among the pleasant shades of the garden until the hour of eating. Having eaten and slept "as they were wont to do", they gather where it pleases the king, and he commands the first discourse to [[Person/neifile]], who begins gladly.

## Framing and Significance

The allusion to "many things concerning their future life" is the first signal of the imminent dissolution of the company, which the king will announce formally at the end of the day; and the day that opens here is the one governed by the theme of liberality and magnificence, fixed by Panfilo the previous evening as a return to the "accustomed law" after the day of free subject.

For the rest the section repeats, for the last time, the customary scansion of the [[DefinedTerm/frame-narrative]] — rising, walk, refreshment at the fountain, meal, rest, gathering and command to the first storyteller — without any variation with respect to the previous days.
