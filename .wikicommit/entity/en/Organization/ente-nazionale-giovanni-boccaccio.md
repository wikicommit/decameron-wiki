The Fondazione Ente Nazionale Giovanni Boccaccio (ENGB) is, at the present day, the principal body in Italy for the promotion of the figure and the work of [[Person/giovanni-boccaccio]]. It has its seat in the house-museum situated at [[Place/certaldo]].

## History

The Foundation was founded in 1958 by presidential decree of 21 May 1958 no. 1154, although the latter came into force only on 27 January 1959. It is at present presided over by the historian of the language and member of the Accademia della Crusca Giovanna Frosini.

## Activities

On the academic side the Foundation promotes the review "Studi sul Boccaccio", founded in 1963 by [[Person/vittore-branca]]; it promotes a summer school, *Accessus a Boccaccio*, devoted to the academic formation of young researchers; and it awards a yearly prize to the best degree and doctoral theses centred upon the figure of the man of Certaldo. It gathers besides in a rich library the works concerning the Boccaccian figure and work, not only European but also extra-European, and preserves some codices of the sixteenth, seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. The ENGB collaborates also with other bodies whose particular purpose is the cult of the figure of Boccaccio, such as the *American Boccaccio Association* (ABA).

Besides its academic activity, the Foundation promotes activities of popularisation through the *Lectura Boccacci*, in collaboration with the Oranona Teatro, staging the tales of the [[Book/decameron]] for the public.
